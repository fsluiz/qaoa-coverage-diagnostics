# Frozen inputs and source geography

The frozen JSON inputs contain the coverage matrices, coordinates, penalty and
executed angles used by the archived analyses. `pef_planilha.xlsx` supplies the
24 surveyed candidate positions used in the mixer-family reconstruction.
Targets in the PEF/Arco benchmark are synthetic; the inputs do not describe routes.

IBGE 2024 municipality-derived coordinates and the simplified outline retain
their source attribution. `ibge_amazonia_legal_2024_provenance.json` records the
official source URLs, original ZIP hashes, coordinate systems and centroid rule.
The original IBGE shapefile ZIPs are not needed for the frozen-input analyses;
their URLs allow independent reconstruction of the derived table.
